A Prayer Over Your Dreams

Father, I lift up Deborah to You — a woman worthy of every good thing she hopes for. Give her the wisdom of her namesake: to judge rightly, to lead boldly, and to rest in Your peace when the battles are done. Grant her favor in every room she enters, strength for every season, and joy that does not depend on circumstances.

May the desires of her heart become milestones behind her, and may every closed door simply be redirection toward something greater. Surround her with people who build her up, and let her shine — like the sun rising in its strength — for everyone blessed enough to stand in her light.
